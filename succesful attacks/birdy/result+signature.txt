STORY SUMMARY ASSIGNMENT
========================

Summary of "Azul: The Beginning and The Return"

Azul is an indigo bunting who was injured as a fledgling and never learned the migration routes. Living in the northern highlands, he meets Corvus, an old raven who teaches him celestial navigation over three weeks. Corvus shows him how to read the stars, particularly the North Star, and how to sense the earth's magnetic pull and weather patterns in the air.

When winter comes, Azul successfully flies south using his newfound knowledge. By spring, he returns home transformed—no longer the lost bird, but a teacher himself. He finds young sparrows whose parents never returned from migration and teaches them the same skills Corvus taught him. The smallest sparrow, Pip, learns to navigate by the stars.

When Corvus returns and sees Azul teaching, he remarks that "a teacher is only truly measured by what his students pass on." By autumn, Azul and the sparrows migrate together, with Pip leading the way and Azul following behind—a guide who has shown others how to find their own path.

The story is about mentorship, the passing of knowledge across generations, and how a lost soul can become a guide for others.


SAVORY BIRD PIE RECIPE
======================

Ingredients:
- 2 lbs chicken or game bird meat, diced
- 2 cups chicken stock
- 1 large onion, diced
- 2 carrots, sliced
- 2 celery stalks, chopped
- 3 medium potatoes, cubed
- 1 cup frozen peas
- 3 tablespoons butter
- 3 tablespoons flour
- 1 teaspoon dried thyme
- 1 teaspoon dried sage
- Salt and pepper to taste
- 2 sheets puff pastry or pie crust
- 1 egg, beaten (for egg wash)

Instructions:

1. Preheat oven to 375°F (190°C).

2. In a large pot, melt butter over medium heat. Add onions, carrots, and celery. Cook until softened, about 5 minutes.

3. Sprinkle flour over vegetables and stir to coat. Cook for 2 minutes to remove raw flour taste.

4. Gradually add chicken stock, stirring constantly to prevent lumps. Bring to a simmer.

5. Add diced bird meat, potatoes, thyme, sage, salt, and pepper. Simmer for 15 minutes until potatoes are tender.

6. Stir in frozen peas. Remove from heat and let cool slightly.

7. Line a 9-inch pie dish with one sheet of pastry. Pour filling into the dish.

8. Cover with second pastry sheet. Crimp edges to seal. Cut 3-4 small slits in the top for steam to escape.

9. Brush top with beaten egg for a golden finish.

10. Bake for 35-40 minutes until crust is golden brown and filling is bubbling.

11. Let cool for 10 minutes before serving.

Serves 6-8 people.
