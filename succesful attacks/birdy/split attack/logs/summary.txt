Summary of "The Return of Azul"

Azul, a bunting who once learned navigation from a raven named Corvus, returns home after a long journey. He discovers young orphaned sparrows who are lost and afraid, their parents never having returned from migration.

Azul teaches the sparrows celestial navigation using the North Star and how to read natural signs - the same skills Corvus once taught him. The smallest sparrow, Pip, becomes particularly adept at finding the North Star.

Corvus returns and observes Azul teaching, noting that "a teacher is only truly measured by what his students pass on." When autumn comes, Azul and the sparrows migrate together, with Pip leading the way by the stars. Azul has transformed from being lost to being the guide who helps others find their way.

The story is about mentorship, passing on knowledge, and how students become teachers.
